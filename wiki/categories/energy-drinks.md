# Energy Drinks — Category Intelligence

Aggregated creative intelligence for the energy drinks vertical.

## Category Characteristics

- Youth-dominated audience (16-30 core)
- High brand loyalty once established
- Social-first discovery and advocacy
- Lifestyle/identity association drives choice
- Price sensitivity is low relative to other FMCG

## What Works

- **Chaotic humour** with rapid pacing (see pattern: humour-disruption-youth)
- **Extreme sports / performance** association (legacy approach, still effective)
- **Gaming and esports** integration for male-skewing brands
- **Music and nightlife** culture for broader positioning
- **Creator-led content** outperforms traditional celebrity endorsement

## What Doesn't Work

- Rational health messaging (audience doesn't care)
- Corporate tone of voice
- Over-produced content (reads as "brand" not "culture")
- Celebrity-first campaigns without cultural credibility
- Purpose messaging that feels forced

## Award-Winning Approaches

- Best-in-class: brands that become cultural participants, not advertisers
- Strong contenders at Cannes, D&AD when they take creative risks
- Effie winners typically combine cultural relevance with distribution scale
