# 60-Second Brand Film Playbook

Playbook for producing high-impact 60-second brand films for TV and digital.

## Pre-Production

### Script Development
- Use the Emotional Arc Framework (5-beat structure)
- Write to a single emotional truth, not a product feature list
- Brief the director on emotion, not execution
- Table-read with non-industry people to test emotional resonance

### Casting
- Authenticity > attractiveness
- Non-actors often deliver more genuine performances
- Chemistry read essential for multi-character spots

### Director Selection
- Match director reel to emotional tone, not just visual style
- Prioritize directors who protect performance over production design
- Consider documentary directors for authenticity-led briefs

## Production

- Shoot 90s+ of material to enable a strong 60s edit
- Capture multiple emotional registers in performance
- Music supervision starts in pre-production, not post
- Plan cutdowns (30s, 15s, 6s) from the start

## Post-Production

- Edit for emotion, not information
- Music drives the edit (not the other way around)
- Grade for mood consistency
- Sound design is 50% of emotional impact
- Test both with and without VO

## Distribution

- TV for reach and brand effect
- Cinema for high-attention impact
- YouTube pre-roll (non-skippable) for digital reach
- Social cutdowns for performance media
