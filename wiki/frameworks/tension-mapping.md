# Cultural Tension Mapping Framework

A systematic approach to identifying and leveraging cultural tensions for creative work.

## Process

### 1. Tension Identification

Map tensions across four layers:
- **Societal**: broad cultural shifts (e.g. individualism vs. community)
- **Category**: industry-specific tensions (e.g. convenience vs. quality in food)
- **Audience**: generational or demographic tensions (e.g. ambition vs. burnout in Gen Z)
- **Brand**: internal contradictions or opportunities (e.g. heritage brand in a youth market)

### 2. Tension Selection

Score each tension on:
- **Resonance**: Does the audience feel this tension?
- **Relevance**: Can the brand credibly participate?
- **Richness**: Is there enough creative material?
- **Risk**: What's the downside if execution falters?

### 3. Creative Translation

- Express the tension as a single provocative sentence
- Find the human story within the tension
- Identify the brand's role: resolver, participant, challenger, or mirror

### 4. Validation

- Test with real audience members (not just strategists)
- Check cultural sensitivity across markets
- Ensure the brand has the right to play in this space

## Common Pitfalls

- Picking a tension the brand has no right to address
- Overstating the brand's role in resolving the tension
- Confusing a category cliche for a genuine cultural tension
- Moving too slowly — tensions have shelf lives
