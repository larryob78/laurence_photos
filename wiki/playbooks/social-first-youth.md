# Social-First Youth Campaign Playbook

A reusable playbook for building social-first campaigns targeting 16-28 year olds.

## Strategy

- Lead with social platforms (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts)
- Build upward to broader channels, not downward from TV
- Community-first: involve audience in creation and distribution
- Speed over polish: reactive content cadence

## Creative Approach

### Tone
- Self-aware, never earnest
- Meme-literate but not try-hard
- Permission to be weird / chaotic
- Low production value = high authenticity signal

### Format Mix
- **Hero**: 1-2 anchor pieces (15-30s) establishing the concept
- **Hub**: 8-12 mid-funnel content pieces expanding the world
- **Hygiene**: Ongoing reactive/community content (3-5 per week)

### Talent
- Creator-led > celebrity-led for this audience
- Micro/mid-tier creators outperform mega-influencers on engagement
- Diversity is expected, not a bonus

## Production

- Shoot ratio: plan for 3-5x the final deliverables
- Capture "behind the scenes" and raw footage for authenticity content
- Platform-native aspect ratios (9:16 primary)
- Subtitle everything — majority consume with sound off

## Measurement

- Engagement rate > reach for optimization
- Share/save ratio as quality signal
- Comment sentiment as brand health proxy
- Earned media amplification as bonus metric

## Budget Guidance

- 60% media on social platforms
- 25% creator fees and content production
- 15% community management and reactive content
