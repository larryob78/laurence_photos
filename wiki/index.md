# Napkin Intelligence Wiki

Central knowledge base for the Napkin Creative Intelligence Flywheel.

## Structure

- **[Patterns](patterns/)** — Generalized creative insights extracted from campaigns and outputs
- **[Categories](categories/)** — Industry and vertical-specific learnings
- **[Brands](brands/)** — Client-specific brand pages (PRIVATE layer)
- **[Campaigns](campaigns/)** — Campaign analysis and performance data
- **[Playbooks](playbooks/)** — Reusable creative playbooks and templates
- **[Frameworks](frameworks/)** — Strategic and creative frameworks

## Layer Separation

| Layer | Scope | Visibility |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Global | Patterns, frameworks, generalized insights | Napkin IP (shared) |
| Category | Industry-specific aggregated learnings | Napkin IP (shared) |
| Client | Brand pages, campaign data, internal notes | Private per-client |

## Rules

1. Never expose client data in global patterns
2. Always abstract insights before promotion to global/category layers
3. Maintain clear boundaries between layers
4. Every insight must be generalized, not client-specific
