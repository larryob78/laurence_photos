# Emotional Arc Framework

A structured approach to building emotional narratives in brand communications.

## The Five-Beat Arc

1. **Hook** (0-5s) — Establish world and character. Create immediate curiosity.
2. **Tension** (5-20s) — Introduce the emotional conflict or desire.
3. **Escalation** (20-40s) — Deepen the stakes. Make the audience care.
4. **Turn** (40-50s) — The moment of resolution, surprise, or catharsis.
5. **Brand Reveal** (50-60s) — Connect the emotion to the brand. Light touch.

## Key Principles

- The brand should feel like a natural part of the resolution, not an interruption
- Music is the single most important production element for emotional impact
- Silence can be more powerful than score at key moments
- Cultural specificity > universal blandness
- Cast for authenticity, not aspiration

## Application

Use this framework when:
- Objective is brand building
- Format is 60s+
- Distribution includes high-attention environments (TV, cinema)

## Variations

- **Comedy Arc**: Replace tension/escalation with setup/misdirection, turn becomes punchline
- **Anthology Arc**: Multiple short vignettes building to a shared theme
- **Documentary Arc**: Real people/stories with minimal scripting
