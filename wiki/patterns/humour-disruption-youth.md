# Chaotic Humour Drives Youth Engagement

High-tempo chaotic humour performs strongly in youth-oriented campaigns when paired with rapid visual escalation and lo-fi production aesthetics.

## Pattern

- Short-form content (< 30s) with rapid cuts
- Self-aware, meme-literate tone
- Deliberately "unpolished" production increases authenticity signals
- Works across social platforms (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)
- Performance amplified when talent mirrors audience demographic

## Evidence

- Multiple Cannes Lions winners in social/digital categories 2022-2025
- D&AD Pencil-winning campaigns in FMCG and entertainment verticals
- Consistent outperformance vs. polished brand films in youth demographics
- IPA Effectiveness data shows higher recall in 18-24 segment

## When to Apply

- Youth audience (16-28)
- Social-first distribution
- Categories: energy drinks, snacks, entertainment, fashion, gaming
- Budget: works at both high and low production budgets

## When NOT to Apply

- Premium/luxury positioning
- Audiences 45+
- Categories requiring trust signals (finance, health)
