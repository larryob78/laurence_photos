# Emotional Storytelling in Long-Form Brand Films

Character-driven emotional narratives in 60-90s formats consistently outperform product-led messaging for brand building metrics (awareness, consideration, affinity).

## Pattern

- Single protagonist with relatable tension
- Brand revealed late (final 20% of runtime)
- Music-led emotional crescendo
- Cinematic production quality signals brand confidence
- Cultural specificity increases resonance (local stories > global templates)

## Evidence

- IPA Effectiveness data: emotional campaigns 2x more effective for long-term brand building
- Cannes Film Lions: 80%+ of Gold+ winners use character-driven narrative
- Effie winners in brand-building categories overwhelmingly use emotional framing

## When to Apply

- Brand-building objectives (not performance/DR)
- TV, cinema, online video (60s+)
- Categories where emotional connection drives purchase
- Sufficient media budget for frequency

## When NOT to Apply

- Short-form social
- Direct response / conversion campaigns
- Categories with rational purchase drivers (B2B, utilities)
